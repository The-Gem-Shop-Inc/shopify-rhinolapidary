# Rhino MVP Sequencing Decision

**Decision date:** 2026-08-27

**Decision owner:** Product Owner

**Status:** Approved sequencing override

## Decision

Epic O is the minimum viable product boundary. The implementation path prioritizes reaching Epic O before spending development capacity on these specifically deferred epics:

- Epic H — Product Discovery, Search, Filters, and Collections: post–Epic O launch.
- Epic I — Machine Comparison and Buying Guidance: post–Epic O launch.
- Epic M — Education and Content Strategy: post–Epic O launch.
- Epic N — Trust, Social Proof, and Conversion Confidence: post–Epic O launch.

This is not a blanket rule that all alphabetically later epics are delayed. The override applies only to H, I, M, and N.

## Existing Handoffs

- D-PBI-016 structured product-role and consumable relationships remain with Epic E and Epic G.
- D-PBI-017 comparison destination, tool, and structured comparison data remain with Epic I, now post–Epic O launch.
- D-PBI-022 broader learning-center and buying-guide expansion remains with Epic M, now post–Epic O launch.
- D-PBI-024 testimonials, reviews, ratings, and social proof remain with Epic N, now post–Epic O launch.
- D-PBI-025 newsletter and institutional lead capture is explicitly deferred post-launch; owned form/process work remains primarily with Epic P.

## Delivery Implication

Deferred work must remain governed and non-rendered where its evidence is incomplete. The deferral does not authorize placeholder content, invented destinations, apps, or tracking.
