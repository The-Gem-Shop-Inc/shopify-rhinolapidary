# Rhino Legal Claims and Attribution Rules

**PBI:** B-033  
**Status:** Approved governance; unresolved claims remain blocked  
**Owner:** Business, Legal, Technical, Product Data, Content, SEO, and Release

## Principle

Absence of evidence is not approval.

## Statuses

- `approved_plain_use`: exact plain wording may be used under the listed
  conditions
- `approved`: legally and operationally approved wording
- `needs_review`: evidence exists but approval is incomplete
- `blocked`: no customer-facing use
- `retired`: wording must be removed or redirected

## Brand names

`Rhino Lapidary` may be used as the operating brand name.

Do not add `®` or `™` until the applicable status and usage have been approved.

`The Everything Machine` may be used as the EM-1 positioning line without a
trademark symbol. It is not the canonical product title.

## Certifications

CE, TSEK, or other certification claims remain blocked until current documents
identify:

- issuing or responsible body
- certified product or component
- applicable configuration
- jurisdiction
- effective date
- expiration or review date
- permitted mark presentation

## Patents

Patent or patent-pending wording remains blocked until the responsible owner,
identifier, jurisdiction, product scope, and current legal status are approved.

## Supplier and manufacturer roles

Do not state manufacturer, exclusive supplier, distributor, fulfillment,
warranty, or repair responsibilities until the entity architecture is approved.

Neutral entity names may appear without role claims.

## Warranty

Do not display a universal warranty duration.

Warranty duration must come from product-specific approved data linked to the
current policy.

## Placement

Legal-sensitive claims require review on:

- homepage
- product pages
- collections
- support
- policies
- social metadata
- email notifications
- structured data
- advertising
- manuals
- video descriptions

## Release requirement

Any change to an approved legal-sensitive claim requires:

- register update
- reviewer approval
- effective date
- review trigger
- admin or code evidence
- release note
- rollback copy

## Completion condition

B-033 closes when:

1. The register exists.
2. Known claims are represented.
3. Blocked wording is machine checked in customer-facing theme files.
4. Every record has an owner and review trigger.
5. Future approvals update the register before implementation.
