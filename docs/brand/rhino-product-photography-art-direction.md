# Rhino Product Photography Art Direction

**PBI:** B-018  
**Status:** Ready for repository adoption; catalog audit still required  
**Primary objective:** Make machinery, included components, compatibility, scale, and condition understandable before purchase

## 1. Assessment of supplied EM-1 advertising

The supplied EM-1 advertisement is useful historical evidence because it shows:

- The complete machine
- A front-oriented view
- Major attachments
- A capability hierarchy
- The legacy red, black, white, burgundy, and gold campaign palette

It is not sufficient as the sole launch product hero because:

- The photograph is embedded in a dense advertising composite.
- The stucco wall and blue cloth create visual noise and color cast.
- The front view alone does not explain depth, rear connections, controls, included components, or scale.
- The image does not clearly distinguish permanently included equipment from staged attachments.

The original photo may remain in historical, About, or campaign archives. A controlled EM-1 reshoot is launch-critical.

## 2. Photography principles

1. Show the complete product before detail crops.
2. Favor evidence over atmosphere.
3. Keep color neutral so machine materials and indicators remain accurate.
4. Show controls, connection points, dimensions, and included parts.
5. Distinguish included, optional, compatible, and demonstration-only items.
6. Avoid props that imply included accessories.
7. Keep safety practices visible in operational images.
8. Use consistent camera height, lighting, and background across machine families.

## 3. Machine shot list

| Priority | Shot | Requirement |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Primary hero | Three-quarter front view, complete machine, neutral background, no crop, controls readable |
| 2 | Straight front | Centered, level, useful for feature orientation and comparison |
| 3 | Left side | Show arbor, wheels, guards, attachments, or access points |
| 4 | Right side | Show corresponding components and connection points |
| 5 | Rear | Show power, plumbing, ventilation, labels, and service access where applicable |
| 6 | Control detail | Switches, speed control, labels, emergency or safety controls |
| 7 | Work-area detail | Wheel, blade, arbor, table, spray, guard, or tool interface |
| 8 | Included contents | Every included component laid out and labeled outside the image |
| 9 | Installed attachment | One image per major supported configuration |
| 10 | Scale and footprint | Product with dimension overlay or approved scale reference |
| 11 | Packaging and delivery | Crate, carton, pallet, or freight condition where relevant |
| 12 | Maintenance | Water system, cleaning access, lubrication, replacement points |
| 13 | In use | Operator performing a representative task with safe posture and PPE |
| 14 | Result | Representative cut, grind, polish, bead, carving, or metalwork output |

## 4. Accessory, replacement-part, and consumable shot list

- Primary isolated product
- Front and back when attachment surfaces differ
- Connector, arbor, thread, mounting, or profile detail
- Packaging and quantity
- Scale reference or dimension diagram
- Compatible-machine context
- Installed view
- Material or grit marking
- Example output only when it does not imply guaranteed results

## 5. Technical capture specification

### Background

Preferred:

- Mist `#F3F4F5`
- Warm White `#FCFBF8`
- Neutral workshop setting for in-use photographs

Avoid:

- Strong colored cloth
- Textured walls
- Busy pegboards
- Unrelated tools
- High-gloss black backgrounds for ordinary catalog media

### Lighting

- Neutral daylight balance near 5000 K
- Soft, even key lighting
- Controlled reflections on metal and painted surfaces
- No dramatic color gels
- Shadows may show form but must not hide controls or labels

### Resolution and format

- Machine master: at least 2400 x 1800 px
- Small product master: at least 2000 px on the longest side
- Color space: sRGB for storefront exports
- Master format: lossless source retained outside the theme repository
- Storefront format: high-quality JPEG, WebP, or Shopify-managed derivative
- Product media belongs in Shopify product media, not theme assets

### Aspect ratios

- Machine gallery: 4:3 preferred
- Product-card crop: 1:1 safe area with at least 8% breathing room
- Detail and installation: 4:3
- Editorial or campaign banner: 16:9
- Social preview: defined later by B-017

## 6. Composition

- Keep the product level and geometrically aligned.
- Leave clear space around controls and attachments.
- Avoid extreme wide-angle distortion.
- Do not crop wheels, guards, motors, feet, or accessory interfaces from the primary image.
- Use consistent orientation across comparable products.
- Keep logos and warning labels readable where resolution permits.
- Do not use red UI-style overlays in the primary product image; reserve them for a separate annotated diagram.

## 7. Alt-text rules

Alt text should identify:

- Product or model
- View or configuration
- Distinguishing component relevant to the image

Examples:

- `Rhino EM-1 machine, three-quarter front view with grinding and polishing wheels installed`
- `Rhino EM-1 rear view showing power and water connections`
- `Six-inch resin bond diamond wheel, mounting side`
- `Brass sprayer installed on the Rhino EM-1 water system`

Avoid:

- “Image of”
- Promotional adjectives
- Repeating every visible label
- Claiming that accessories are included unless confirmed

## 9. Shopify admin audit instructions

This is an inspection task. Do not replace product media during B-018.

1. Open each launch-critical product in Shopify admin.
2. Record each media item in the gap register.
3. Confirm image dimensions, order, alt text, and whether the image depicts included or optional equipment.
4. Check the first image at square, 4:3, and narrow mobile crops.
5. Record duplicates, obsolete branding, unreadable text, and external-host dependencies.
6. Create separate admin implementation PBIs for approved uploads, reordering, or alt-text changes.
7. Record every later product-media change with an admin change record and release note.

## 10. Completion condition

B-018 can close when the guide is adopted and the launch-critical product gap register is populated. It does not require every reshoot to be completed.
